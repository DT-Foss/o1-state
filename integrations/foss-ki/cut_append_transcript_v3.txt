The story: forgetting means cutting ALL evidence segments for a
relationship, not just the ones from the source you happen to be
thinking about. find_segments_citing(subject, obj) lets a caller
find every segment citing a given directed edge; a caller building
a real 'forget this relationship' feature must query BOTH
(subject, obj) and (obj, subject) and union the results, because
different converted sources can and do encode the same real-world
relationship in different directions with different mechanism labels.
